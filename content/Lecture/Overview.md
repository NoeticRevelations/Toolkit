# Introduction to the whole course 
- [l] This training was made because of the growing complexities and co-morbidities in the population. There was also a growing expectation that both should be managed. 
	- See the NHS 10 Year Health Plan
	- The [IAPT LTC/MUS Guidance (2018)](https://www.england.nhs.uk/north-west/wp-content/uploads/sites/48/2019/04/IAPT-LTC-_Full_Implementation_Guidance.pdf)
	- IAPT Curriculum for PWPs doing LTC Training (2022)

## Focus of the Curriculum 
There are 7 types of main LTC areas currently:
- Diabetes (T1&2)
- Cardiac Conditions
- Respiratory Conditions
- Cancer with a positive prognosis 
- Chronic Fatigue Syndrome 
- Neurological Conditions 
- Long-Covid 

Training will look a general principles and adaptations rather than disorder specific approaches.  


> [!attention]+ Course Aims
> Learn how to adapt your PWP skills to better support patients who have both mental health problems and distressing long-term physical conditions.
> 
> Core Focus Areas:
> - **Knowledge**: Understand the relationship between physical and psychological health.
> - **Adaptation**: Adjust your assessment and self-management techniques for physical symptoms. 
> - **Competency**: Build practical skills through roleplay and evidence-based guidelines
